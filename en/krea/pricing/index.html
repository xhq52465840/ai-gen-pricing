What to know before you pay

  • Extra compute packs are one-time purchases and expire after 90 days. Source
  • After your units run out you can keep generating in relaxed mode, but only with supported in-house image models. Source
  • Krea publishes generation counts per plan rather than units per model. We only list a model where Krea states a count, for example 450 Nano Banana 2 generations on Pro. Source
