What to know before you pay

  • Unused credits don’t roll over. Monthly plans refresh on your billing date; annual plans refresh every 30 days. Source
  • If a generation fails, credits are usually returned automatically. A finished generation is not refunded, even if you don’t like the result. Source
  • “Unlimited” models only work on higgsfield.ai (not MCP, CLI, Canvas or Supercomputer) and may be slowed down at busy times. Source
  • Starter only includes selected models (Seedance 2.0 Fast and Mini among them), so the calculator uses Plus or Ultra for the models on this site. Source
