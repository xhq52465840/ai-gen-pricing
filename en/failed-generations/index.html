What failed generations cost you

List prices are per generation, not per clip you can use. Two different things cost you money: errors (the job fails or is blocked) and rejects (it finishes, but the face drifts, the text is wrong or the motion is off, so you run it again).

The maths

Real price per usable output = list price ÷ keep rate. Keep one in two and you pay double; keep one in four and you pay four times. Errors that the platform refunds don’t count, but rejects always do.

Example: Kling 4.0 at 1080p is $0.235 per second through the official API, so a 5 s clip is $1.18. If you keep one clip in three, each clip you publish costs $3.53.

What the platforms say

  • If a generation fails, credits are usually returned automatically. A finished generation is not refunded, even if you don’t like the result. Source
  • Subscription credits can be refunded within 3 days of your first purchase if you meet the eligibility rules. Source
  • Add-on credits don’t expire and stay usable after your subscription ends, but they are not refundable. Source
  • Extra compute packs are one-time purchases and expire after 90 days. Source
  • Prepaid resource packs are also sold; they are non-refundable and expire 90 days after purchase. Source
  • Standard and Pro credits reset each month; Max rolls over up to one month of unused credits. Extra credits you buy never expire. Source
  • Premium+ and higher include unlimited generations on some models (Nano Banana 2 and Nano Banana Pro among them), which can make those models free at the margin. Source
  • Runway: 80-credit minimum per generation (Seedance 2.5), 56-credit minimum per generation (Seedance 2.0). Source

How to waste less

  • Draft cheap, finish expensive: test prompts at 480p or with a fast/lite variant, then render the keeper at full quality.
  • Generate the shortest clip that works; most video models bill per second.
  • Lock the image first: an image-to-video job from a good still is rejected less often than text-to-video.
  • Check whether moderation blocks are charged before you run a batch.
