OpenArt pricing explained

OpenArt sells monthly credit plans. Below: what each plan costs, what one credits is worth in dollars, and what the models we track really cost per second or per image.

Checked: 28 Oct 2026

OpenArt alternativesOfficial pricing page
OpenArt does not publish a per-model price for the models we track, so it is not in the calculator. Its plans are listed so you can compare credit value once you see the cost in the app.

What to know before you pay

  • Plans are sold in credits per month, with rough guides such as “up to ~4,000 images or ~50 videos” on Starter. Source
  • The pricing page doesn’t list a credit cost per model, so OpenArt isn’t in the calculator. Source
  • Current promotion on the page: 50% off Kling 4.0 generations on Wonder or Pro until 20 Nov. Source
