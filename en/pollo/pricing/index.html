Pollo AI pricing explained

Pollo AI sells monthly credit plans. Below: what each plan costs, what one credits is worth in dollars, and what the models we track really cost per second or per image.

Checked: 28 Oct 2026

Pollo AI alternativesOfficial pricing page
Pollo AI does not publish a per-model price for the models we track, so it is not in the calculator. Its plans are listed so you can compare credit value once you see the cost in the app.

What to know before you pay

  • Unused subscription credits don’t carry over to the next billing cycle. Source
  • Subscription credits can be refunded within 3 days of your first purchase if you meet the eligibility rules. Source
  • Add-on credits don’t expire and stay usable after your subscription ends, but they are not refundable. Source
  • The pricing page shows plan totals (“up to 80 videos/month” on Lite) but no credit cost per model, so Pollo isn’t in the calculator. Source
