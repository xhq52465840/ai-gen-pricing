About GenCost and how we check prices

GenCost is an independent price comparison for AI image and video generation. We are not paid by, owned by or partnered with any platform on this site.

Our rules

  • Every price comes from the platform’s own pricing page, model page or API documentation, and links to it.
  • Every price carries the date we read it. All prices on this version were checked on 28 Oct 2026.
  • We never guess. If a platform does not publish a price for a model, that platform is left out for that model, even when third-party sites quote a number.
  • Credit prices are converted to dollars with the plan’s own price and credit allowance. When we work a number out (for example from “450 generations per plan”), we say so next to it.
  • Promotions are shown as notes, not as the price, because they end.
