Midjourney pricing explained

Midjourney sells monthly credit plans. Below: what each plan costs, what one GPU hours is worth in dollars, and what the models we track really cost per second or per image.

Checked: 28 Oct 2026

Midjourney alternativesOfficial pricing page
Midjourney does not publish a per-model price for the models we track, so it is not in the calculator. Its plans are listed so you can compare credit value once you see the cost in the app.

What to know before you pay

  • Plans sell Fast GPU time, not images: 3.3 hours on Basic up to 60 hours on Mega. Extra Fast time is $4 an hour. Source
  • Standard, Pro and Mega include unlimited Relax-mode images; Pro and Mega add unlimited SD video in Relax mode. Source
  • Midjourney publishes no per-image price, so it isn’t in the calculator. Companies with more than $1M a year in revenue must use Pro or Mega. Source
