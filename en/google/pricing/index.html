What to know before you pay

  • Veo 3.1 preview models in the Gemini API were shut down on 22 Oct 2026; Google points developers to Gemini Omni Flash or Veo on its enterprise platform. Veo 3.1 is still sold through fal, Replicate and Runway. Source
  • Gemini Omni Flash 1.1 video is billed in tokens: $17.50 per million output tokens at 5,792 tokens per second of 720p, about $0.10 a second. Source
  • Image prices are token-based too: a 1K Nano Banana 2 image uses 1,120 tokens, $0.067. Batch mode halves the price. Source
