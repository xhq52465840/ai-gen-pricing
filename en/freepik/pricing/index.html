What to know before you pay

  • Freepik’s pricing page now redirects to Magnific, the new name of its creative suite. Source
  • Annual plans give a yearly credit pot (240,000 on Premium) that is valid for a year with no monthly reset. We divide it by 12 to compare. Prices exclude VAT. Source
  • Premium+ and higher include unlimited generations on some models (Nano Banana 2 and Nano Banana Pro among them), which can make those models free at the margin. Source
