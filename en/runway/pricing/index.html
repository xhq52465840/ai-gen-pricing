What to know before you pay

  • The API (Runway Dev) sells credits at $0.01 each, and also resells Kling, Seedance, Veo, Wan and Gemini models. Source
  • Standard and Pro credits reset each month; Max rolls over up to one month of unused credits. Extra credits you buy never expire. Source
  • You can buy more credits from the dashboard; the minimum purchase is 1,000 credits. Source
